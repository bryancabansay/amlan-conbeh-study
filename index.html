Why coldness matters more in the story

Survey: coldness = 3.43 / 5. Interviews: 5 of 6 mentioned it.

Context effect

“Cold” becomes important at the moment of consumption.

Heat, work, travel, exercise, meals, and immediate thirst can change which attribute becomes most important. The survey captures stated importance; interviews capture the purchase situation.

Attitude-behavior gap

Sustainability is liked, but often not decisive.

Respondents can genuinely support eco-friendly packaging while still choosing based on price, availability, coldness, safety, or convenience when the purchase happens.

The retailer is part of the choice architecture

In low-involvement purchases, the retailer determines what is stocked, cold, visible, easy to reach, and sometimes what is physically handed to the buyer.

What the data implies for management

These are evidence-based implications, not proof of causality.

1 · Fix access first

Make Amlan easy for retailers to order and replenish.

A clear retailer contact channel, reliable delivery, and stock tracking address friction before the consumer ever reaches the product.

2 · Win the moment

Visible, cold, easy to reach, easy to hand over.

For fast, low-involvement purchases, cooler execution may matter as much as communication.

3 · Segment the journey

Do not treat every bottled-water buyer the same.

Single-bottle buyers, household bulk buyers, and institutional/event buyers can have different buying DNA and different priorities.

Research basis

Bottled Water Buying Journey Survey · 6 consumer interviews · 6 retailer interviews · 13 store observations · 1 Amlan company interview · group coding and triangulation.

Framework lens: Martyn R. Lewis, How Customers Buy… & Why They Don’t.

Interpret carefully

Exploratory convenience sample. Findings describe the participants and observed outlets in this project. They should not be generalized to all Filipino consumers or all retailers.
